Cookie preference

The native application should not invent a cookie layer. If the separate website later uses analytics, advertising, embedded media, or other non-essential technologies, this page should expose a proper preference center and the privacy policy should describe it accurately.

Consent records

For account or legal acceptance flows, store the policy version and acceptance timestamp when required. Do not silently treat a page view as consent.
