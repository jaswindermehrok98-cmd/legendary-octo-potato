ACCESSIBILITY

Quiet interface.
Clear access.

OFFLINE OS should remain usable while staying visually minimal. The launcher is designed around readable type, clear labels, sufficient touch targets, semantic controls, and reduced motion.

Motion

A Reduced Motion setting is available for non-essential animations.

Assistive technology

Controls should expose meaningful labels and semantics for Android accessibility services such as TalkBack.

Contrast and touch

Core text and actions use strong contrast and practical touch areas rather than relying on color alone.
