COMPATIBILITY

Android first.
Capabilities vary.

OFFLINE OS is a native Android launcher. Some features depend on Android version, device manufacturer behavior, and permissions or special access granted by the user.

Core launcher

Home role, installed-app discovery, application launching, search, local settings, and the core offline experience are designed as launcher fundamentals.

Special capabilities

Notification Inbox depends on notification access. Usage-based limits depend on usage access. App intervention may depend on an explicitly enabled accessibility-based service. Device locking depends on an eligible Android device-management configuration.

Graceful degradation

When a capability is unavailable, the launcher should remain usable and explain the missing capability instead of pretending a feature is active.
