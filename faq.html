FAQ

Is it a real launcher? — Yes. It is designed to serve as an Android Home application.
Is it a new Android operating system? — No. It runs on Android.
Does it require internet for core launcher functions? — The core product is designed to remain useful offline.
Can it reply to every message? — No. Reply is only available when the originating notification exposes a supported Android action.
Can it force-close every limited app? — Enforcement depends on Android capabilities and device configuration.
Is usage measurement identical on every phone? — Android platform behavior can vary by device and version.
