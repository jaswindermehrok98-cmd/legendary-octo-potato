What the launcher handles

Depending on features you enable, OFFLINE OS may interact with installed-app information, notification metadata, Android usage information, launcher preferences, timers, limits, and local experimental content.

Local-first

The core launcher is designed to perform locally. Notification contents, app usage information, and THE READ answers should not be uploaded by default.

Account data

If an account system is enabled, account information such as email and profile details may be processed by the configured authentication provider. Do not enable cloud sync for sensitive launcher data without implementing and documenting it explicitly.

Permissions

Special Android capabilities such as Home role, notification access, usage access, accessibility-based intervention, and device-management access are requested only for the features that need them. Android controls whether those capabilities are actually granted.

Third-party apps

UNPLUG does not own the content produced by third-party applications such as messaging, email, or social apps. Where the launcher handles notification metadata from those apps, it is acting as a presentation layer on the user's device.

Retention and deletion

Local data can be cleared from the launcher settings. Any server-side account data must have a documented retention and deletion process before public launch.
